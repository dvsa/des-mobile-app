Stop-drive order in effect Some Citroën, DS and Vauxhall vehicles are affected by a Stellantis 'stop-drive' order:
  • Citroën C3 (2008-2017)
  • Citroën DS3 (2008-2019)
  • Citroën C-Zero (2010-2016)
  • Peugeot iOn (2010-2016)
  • Citroën C4 (2009-2018)
  • DS 4 (2010-2018)
  • Vauxhall Astra (5th gen or ‘H’ version) (2005-2013)
  • Vauxhall Astravan (5th gen or ‘H’ version) (2005-2013)
  • Vauxhall Astra Twin Top (2005 to 2012)
  • Vauxhall Vectra (2005-2008)
  • Vauxhall Signum (2005-2008)
If a car is being used for a test could be affected, use the recall checker for the vehicle manufacturer:
  • Citroën recall checker
  • DS recall checker
  • Vauxhall recall checker
If the recall check shows that:
  • the repair has been completed; carry out the test as normal
  • the repair has not been completed; do not take the car out on test
